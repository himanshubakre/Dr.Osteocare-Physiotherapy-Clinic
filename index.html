About the clinic

Care built around how your body actually moves.

Dr.Osteocare Physiotherapy Clinic sits in Bail Bazar, Kalyan West — a dedicated space for assessment, hands-on treatment and structured rehabilitation. Led by Dr. Hassan Hajwani, the clinic combines manual therapy with evidence-based exercise programs to treat the cause of pain, not just the symptom.

With close to a decade of clinical experience and registration with the Maharashtra State Council for Occupational Therapy & Physiotherapy, the team works with athletes, post-surgical patients and anyone dealing with persistent joint, back or neck pain — including home visits for patients who can't travel to the clinic.

FAQ

Good to know

What is the consultation fee?

The initial consultation is priced at approximately ₹300. Follow-up session pricing is discussed during your visit.

Do you offer home-visit physiotherapy?

Yes — home-visit sessions are available for patients who are immobile or unable to travel to the clinic. Message us on WhatsApp to arrange one.

What are your working hours?

The clinic is open all seven days, 9:00 AM to 11:00 PM.

Do I need a doctor's referral?

No referral is required — you can walk in or book directly for an initial assessment.

Is the clinic easy to find?

Yes, it's on Valipeer Road, Bail Bazar, Kalyan West, opposite JP Resort / Daire Clinic. See the map below for directions.
